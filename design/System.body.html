242 declarations at 9 and 10px were the real base of the old design — that is what made it read as an admin panel. Shrinking text to fit more in is not density; cutting content is.

And nothing shouts. Seven uppercase label classes with 23 letter-spacings became one: 12px, medium, sentence case, no tracking. Headings are 15px semibold — larger than the body they sit above, which was not previously true.

“Discount” used to sit at hue 20 — two degrees from bad — so a topic chip read as an alert on the one screen where red means blocked or live. Topics now start at 105 and step by 30–40°, and other carries no hue at all, because it is not a topic.

52px rail · 28px live strip · 44px content bar · 34px sub-nav · 420px inspector. Navigation spends width, never height — the console cannot spare the vertical, and it was previously giving up 92px of chrome plus 216px of section bars.

Input and focus — the two the sheet was missing
rest focused that regex does not compile disabled · needs a live adapter

Keyboard focus is 2px accent, offset 1 on every control, and it is the same ring a focused proposal card carries — the console is driven from the keyboard, so focus is not decoration.

The rule behind all of it

The console is read in peripheral vision by someone talking to a camera. Colour means state, position means category, and nothing moves unless something changed. Every number that could be mistaken for a promise carries the version it was read at — and every element that can be explained opens the same inspector, never a fifth kind of popover.
