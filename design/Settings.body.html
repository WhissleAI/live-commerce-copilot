What the copilot may use

Each source off is one class of question it will abstain on rather than guess at. The consequence is printed beside the switch, because that is the thing worth knowing before turning one off.

Buyer chatRequired. Without it there is nothing to answer.
Host audio, with emotion and intentOff means it never hears “last one in this waist” — the lot facts you say out loud and never typed. On, the audio is kept in chunks beside its transcript so the show can be played back.
Camera framesA frame every 12 seconds, used to name a placeholder lot and read a price card. The frames the agent reads are kept with their reading for the report's timeline.
Sold comps and web researchPowers the research card. Never used to ground a reply on its own — a web page is not your listing.
Prior shows' answered questionsReuses what you already answered well. Off if you would rather each show start clean.

eBay

Two capabilities, not one. Searching eBay and reading its catalog needs an application key; acting on your listings needs you to sign in and consent, and no key can stand in for that. The eBay Live session — what lets Discover see the grid — is a third thing, signed into once from the terminal and never typed for you.

Reading the catalogsandboxworking
  • Browse live listings behind the research card's comparables
  • Taxonomy the fields eBay requires per category, checked against your catalog on Setup
  • Sold prices what things actually went for, over the last 90 days — preferred over asking prices everywhere. Sandbox carries no sales history, so lookups there fall through to active listings and say so
