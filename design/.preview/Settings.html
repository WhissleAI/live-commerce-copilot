What the copilot may use

Each source off is one class of question it will abstain on rather than guess at. The consequence is printed beside the switch, because that is the thing worth knowing before turning one off.

Buyer chatRequired. Without it there is nothing to answer.
Host audio, with emotion and intentOff means it never hears “last one in this waist” — the lot facts you say out loud and never typed.
Camera framesA frame every 12 seconds, used to name a placeholder lot and read a price card. Frames are analysed and discarded, never stored.
Your catalog and policiesOnly offered on a show you own. A stranger's show grounds in the stream alone.
Sold comps and web researchPowers the research card. Never used to ground a reply on its own — a web page is not your listing.
Prior shows' answered questionsReuses what you already answered well. Off if you would rather each show start clean.
